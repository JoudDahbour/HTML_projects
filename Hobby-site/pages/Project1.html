Project Details

Status: Completed!

This project lets you play Flappy Bird by flexing your arm instead of clicking. Electrodes pick up the signal from your muscle, and the circuit turns each flex into a mouse click that makes the bird jump.

Components:

  • Instrumentation amplifier
  • Band-pass filter
  • Integrator
  • 555 timer one-shot
  • Optoisolator
